This will be considered that u game from the first player who threw the 9 Wan, even if u forgot. Cos there is a common convention that even if a person has forgotten to game, he can still game with any of the discards (priority is still from the player to your right and start from there) as long as it is not your turn yet. Hence u have 3 turns before your own turn and cannot game anymore from the other players' discards.
